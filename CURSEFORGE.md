**Free a bag slot in one click.**

Bagnon Cheapest adds a button to your Bagnon bag window that finds your lowest-value stack: grey items first, then by vendor price × stack size.

**Features**
- Hover to see the next four candidates. Every other slot darkens so the target stands out.
- Shift-click the button to destroy that stack, or sell it if a vendor is open. Shift-right-click skips it until you close your bags.
- Click the button to open a list of every candidate. Shift-click an item there to destroy or sell it, or right-click to exclude it for good.
- Exclusions are shared across all your characters.

**Options** (Options → AddOns, or `/bcheap options`)
- The button offers grey items only by default. Raise its quality limit up to Epic.
- Rank purely by vendor value instead of putting greys first.

**Safe by default**
- The slot is checked again before anything happens.
- Uncommon and better items always ask for Blizzard's "type DELETE" confirmation.
- Never acts in combat. Quest items and items with no sell price are never listed.

**Commands:** `/bcheap` opens the list, `/bcheap scan` prints the ranking to chat, and `/bcheap options` opens the options.

Requires **Bagnon**. Built for WoW Forever.
