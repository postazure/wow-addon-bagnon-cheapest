# Changelog

## Unreleased

## 1.1.0

- Shift-right-click the bag button to skip the item it shows. It comes back
  the next time you open your bags, and isn't added to your exclusions.

## 1.0.0

First release, for WoW Forever 1.60.1.

- A button on Bagnon's bag window shows your lowest-value stack and its
  vendor price. Grey items come first.
- Hover it to see the next four, with every other slot darkened.
- Shift-click to destroy that stack, or sell it when a vendor is open.
- Click to open a list of every candidate. Right-click to exclude an item
  for all your characters.
